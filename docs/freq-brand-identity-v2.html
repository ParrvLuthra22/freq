01 — The Essence

FREQ reads who you actually are at 2am — not the version you'd write in a bio. Your taste is the most honest thing about you, and it flirts before you say a word.

Four instincts meet here: Hinge's warm editorial restraint, Apple Watch's quiet precision, Cosmos's gallery-grade curation, and the ease of a good text thread. Dark, warm, confident. It flirts through wit and specificity, never volume. Nothing shouts. The luxury is in the restraint.

02 — The Mark

One glyph: a dial that's
also a waveform.

The mark is a miniature of the signature — a precision ring holding a single frequency wave. Logo and app icon are the same object, so the brand is recognisable at 24px on a home screen and at billboard scale. Rose on ink is primary; it inverts cleanly to ink on cream for light surfaces.

The rule: light mode isn't a colour swap, it's a tone swap. Cream replaces ink, ink-text replaces ivory, and both accents darken so they hold contrast on paper — rose to Signal Deep, champagne to Bronze.

Dark stays the default and the hero — it's the 2am brand. Light is the polite daytime twin for share cards, email, and anyone who lives in light mode. The editorial serif and the mark carry across untouched, so it always reads as FREQ.

06 — The Signature

The FREQ Dial.

Apple-Watch DNA × frequency

One object carries the whole brand: a precision dial borrowed from Apple Watch, wrapped in the sine-wave that gives FREQ its name. It's the compatibility score, the loading state, the profile centrepiece, and the shareable card — all the same shape. The waveform repeats everywhere as a hairline divider and texture, so the frequency motif is felt long before it's noticed.

07 — Voice & Tone

Flirts through wit, never volume.

We sound like

  • Specific and knowing — name the artist, the hour, the rare overlap.
  • Playfully bold, a little teasing, always in on the joke.
  • Warm and adult. Confident enough to be brief.
  • Sentence case. Real punctuation. A well-placed em dash.

We never sound like

  • Thirsty, crude, or trying too hard.
  • Generic dating-app filler — "Hey, you two matched!"
  • Loud, emoji-stuffed, or exclamation-heavy.
  • Judgmental about anyone's taste. Cheeky, not mean.
